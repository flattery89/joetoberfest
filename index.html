Beer & Food

Three kegs of it were brewed in this house. Joe and Destinee are handling the food.

Vom FassBrewed by Joe

  • KölschCrisp, clean, gone by three. The easy one.
  • American Pale AleHoppy and bright. The one that isn't German, and we're fine with that.
  • Roasted StoutDark, roasty, for later in the day when you've slowed down.
  • Possibly a fourthA commercial keg may join the lineup. Undecided.

Three kegs of homebrew is a lot of grain and a lot of Sundays. If you'd like to chip in toward the beer, it's appreciated — and genuinely not expected.

Aus der KücheJoe & Destinee

  • WurstMade here, off the grill, with good mustard.
  • Bavarian PretzelsMade here too. Warm and salted, worth timing your arrival around.
  • Sides & dessertsWhatever everyone brings — see below.

That's the whole plan, food-wise. Wurst and pretzels from us, the rest from you if you're up for it.

Was mitbringen?

What to Bring & What to Wear

Bring a side or a dessert

If you can, bring a side or a dessert — it's appreciated, and it's not required. Come empty-handed and you'll still be fed. Wurst, pretzels, and beer are covered either way.

If you do bring something, say what when you RSVP so we don't end up with nine potato salads.

Already claimed:

  • German potato salad
  • German butter cake

Still wide open: apple strudel, spätzle, cucumber salad, obatzda, black forest cake, a pretzel board, or a bag of ice — genuinely, someone please bring ice.

Dress code: Tracht optional

Lederhosen and dirndls enthusiastically encouraged and in no way required. Nobody will be turned away in jeans.

  • Lederhosen, dirndl, or a plaid shirt and confidence
  • Sunscreen — it's a Florida afternoon in September
  • Your own stein if you own one and want to show off
  • Kids are welcome. Bring whatever keeps them happy.

One actual event: the stein hold

Later in the evening, when enough people have opinions about their own arm strength, we settle it. Full stein, arm straight out, last one holding wins. That's the only thing on the schedule.

Even the locals are dressing up.
