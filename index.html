Speisekarte

Beer & Food

Everything below is on the house. What you bring is dessert and sides — see the next bit.

Vom FassFrom the tap

  • MärzenThe real thing. Amber, malty, dangerous.
  • HefeweizenBanana, clove, served with a lemon you should refuse.
  • FestbierLighter, crisper, deceptively drinkable.
  • RadlerBeer and lemon soda. Pace yourself, or don't.
  • Not beerWine, cider, soda, and cold water for the wise.

Aus der KücheFrom the kitchen

  • Bratwurst & KnackwurstOff the grill, on a roll, with good mustard.
  • Soft PretzelsWarm, salted, with beer cheese.
  • Sauerkraut & Red CabbageBoth. Don't make it a whole thing.
  • SchnitzelPounded thin, fried gold, squeeze of lemon.
  • KartoffelsalatGerman potato salad. Warm, vinegary, correct.

Was mitbringen?

What to Bring & What to Wear

Bring a dish

It's a potluck for sides and desserts — the beer, brats, and pretzels are covered. Tell us what you're bringing when you RSVP so we don't end up with nine potato salads.

Already claimed:

  • German potato salad
  • German butter cake

Ideas still wide open: apple strudel, spätzle, cucumber salad, obatzda, black forest cake, a pretzel board, or just a bag of ice — genuinely, someone please bring ice.

Dress code: Tracht optional

Lederhosen and dirndls enthusiastically encouraged and in no way required. Nobody will be turned away in jeans.

  • Lederhosen, dirndl, or a plaid shirt and confidence
  • A jacket — we'll be outside once the sun drops
  • Your own stein if you own one and want to show off
  • Kids are welcome. Bring whatever keeps them happy.
